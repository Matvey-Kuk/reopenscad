    Multi-part designs

    Every top-level object is its own part: own colour, show/hide, STL or 3MF export, and clearance checks against the others.

    evaluate(design, checks: ["supports"])
    • ✓5 parts, all closed solids
    • ✓Bounds 90 × 227 × 118 mm
    • ✓Tightest gap 0.4 mm, tolerance 0.2
    • !Shelf overhangs 42.5 mm: needs support
    measurecheck_intersectionsrender_imagediffset_parameters

    Tools for agents to check their work

    Over MCP an agent measures parts, finds collisions and tolerance misses, renders views, diffs revisions and checks printability.
